Deep Dive: Lear Corp ↘

Lear Corporation is a global automotive technology leader in Seating and E-Systems, providing complete seat systems and electrical architectures to major automakers.

Palantir Foundry is an advanced enterprise data platform designed to integrate complex data sources, build robust business ontologies, and power operational analytics.

Project Highlights: During my internship at Lear Corporation, I led a real-world data automation project focused on the Key Operating Trends (KOT) data analysis process using Palantir Foundry. I successfully automated the manual filtering and visualization of KOT data by building robust data pipelines using Pipeline Builder. Furthermore, I created a comprehensive business Ontology that served as the foundation for a newly developed interactive dashboard. This project allowed me to acquire extensive hands-on experience in advanced data visualization, pipeline automation, and the use of enterprise-level tools. It also provided a deep understanding of complex enterprise data structures, continuous integration (CI) processes, and the importance of efficient cross-functional collaboration.
